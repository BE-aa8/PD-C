Adaptations (by tools/build_pancreas_mesh.mjs in this repository): duplicate vertices merged;
coordinates re-centred on the pancreas, scaled (1 unit = 50 mm) and rotated to a y-up frame;
hotspot anchor points and schematic islet points added. These adapted files are shared under
